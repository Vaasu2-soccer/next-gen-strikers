# NEXT GEN STRIKERS: THE FUTURE OF FOOTBALL

A high-quality 3D third-person soccer game featuring realistic player movement, fluid animations, advanced ball physics, and immersive stadium environments.

## Core Features

### Gameplay Systems
- **4v4 & 8v8 Multiplayer**: Private, Casual, and Ranked modes
- **Unlockable Styles**: Custom playstyles with 3 unique abilities (15s, 30s, 45s cooldowns)
- **Awakening System**: Special moveset replacements with cinematic activation
- **Flow State Mechanic**: Build flow through skillful play, spin for buffs at 35% threshold
- **Ball Physics**: Realistic ball dynamics and control mechanics
- **Player Animations**: Fluid, responsive character movement and interactions

### Progression & Ranking
- **Ranked Tiers**: Rookie → Sophomore → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Champion → Grand Champion → Elite Baller → Street Ball Master → The God of Football
- **Star System**: Win-based progression with increasing requirements per rank
- **Character Customization**: Unlockable styles, abilities, and cosmetics

### Economy & Monetization
- **Daily Rewards**: 1500 Bucks per claim
- **Gacha Spins**: Multiple spin types (Standard, Lucky, Ultra, Infinity) with rarity system
- **Style & Flow Shops**: Separate purchasable content
- **Global Announcements**: Rare roll celebrations

### Rarity System
11 main rarities + 2 special categories with unique VFX, SFX, and drop rates
