# NEXT GEN STRIKERS: THE FUTURE OF FOOTBALL
## Game Design Document

### 1. Overview
NEXT GEN STRIKERS is a high-quality 3D third-person soccer game with realistic player movement, fluid animations, advanced ball physics, and immersive stadium environments. The gameplay emphasizes skill expression, strategic teamwork, and competitive ranked play.

### 2. Core Systems

#### 2.1 Gameplay Mechanics
- **Player Movement**: Responsive, skill-based controls with realistic animations
- **Ball Physics**: Advanced physics system with realistic ball dynamics
- **Skill-Based Moves**: Players can learn and execute advanced techniques
- **Team Coordination**: 4v4 and 8v8 multiplayer matches

#### 2.2 Style System
- **Unlockable Styles**: 16+ unique playstyles
- **Ability Set**: Each style includes 3 abilities with 15s, 30s, and 45s cooldowns
- **Awakening System**: Special powerful moveset that replaces normal abilities
- **Cinematic Activation**: Awakening activations feature unique cutscenes

#### 2.3 Flow State System
- **Flow Bar**: Builds from successful actions, possession, skill moves, team plays
- **Activation Threshold**: 35% flow bar required
- **Spin Mechanic**: Wheel spin selects 1-4 random attribute buffs
- **Buff Range**: 5% to 75% enhancement
- **Duration**: 10-30 seconds per buff
- **Buff Types**:
  - Ball Control: Enhanced ball accuracy
  - Movement Speed: Increased player velocity
  - Shooting Precision: More accurate shots
  - Tackle Strength: More effective defensive plays
  - Creative Passing: Special pass types

#### 2.4 Ranked System
- **12 Ranks**: Rookie → Sophomore → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Champion → Grand Champion → Elite Baller → Street Ball Master → The God of Football
- **Star Progression**: 
  - Rookie-Diamond: Win 3-5 games for 1 star
  - Champion-StreetBallMaster: Win 10 games for 1 star
  - The God of Football: No further progression
- **5 Stars = Rank Up**: Complete all 5 stars to advance to next rank

#### 2.5 Economy System
- **Bucks**: In-game currency earned through matches and daily rewards
- **Crystals**: Premium currency for cosmetics
- **Daily Gift**: 1500 Bucks per claim
- **Spin System**: Multiple spin tiers with increasing costs and better odds

#### 2.6 Rarity System
16 total rarities with unique VFX, SFX, and drop rates:
- Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary (Standard spin only)
- Mythic (Standard+)
- Divine, Godly (Lucky+)
- Transcendent, Secret, Infinity, Celestial, Omniversal (Ultra+)
- Best in History, Eternal Apex, Absolute Sovereign (Infinity+)
- Plus 2 hidden categories

### 3. User Interface

#### 3.1 Home Menu
- **Black Background**: Professional, modern aesthetic
- **Title**: "NEXT GEN STRIKERS: THE FUTURE OF FOOTBALL"
- **Title Effects**: Blue flashing every 2-3 seconds with lightning VFX
- **Authentication**: Username/Striker ID + Password login
- **Main Options**: Play, Shop, Profile, Daily Gift

#### 3.2 Game Mode Selection
- **Match Type**: Private, Casual, Ranked
- **Team Size**: 4v4 or 8v8
- **Quick Match**: Matchmaking for casual and ranked

#### 3.3 Shop Interface
- **Spin System**: Multiple tier options
- **Style Shop**: Purchase or unlock styles
- **Flow Shop**: Acquire flow modifiers
- **Cosmetics**: Player customization items

### 4. Progression
